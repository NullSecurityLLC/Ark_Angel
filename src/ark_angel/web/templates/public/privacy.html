How we handle what you send

Privacy

If you send a tip

  • Your tip goes to the people working that case: the family or their representative, and anyone they've shared the case with. It is never shown publicly.
  • Contact details are optional. If you give them, only the case team sees them.
  • We don't store your IP address. To stop spam we keep a scrambled code made from it, which can't be turned back into the address, and we delete it after 24 hours.
  • If you attach a photo, the location, time and phone model saved inside it are kept, because they help place a sighting. Only the case team can see the photo. Remove location from a photo before sending if you'd rather not share it.

Public profiles

  • A profile goes public only after an administrator reviews it, and only for someone reported missing to police.
  • Profile photos are re-saved without any hidden data, so they can't reveal where they were taken.
  • A profile comes down as soon as the person is found or the family asks.

Accounts

  • We store your username, a scrambled form of your password (never the password itself), and a log of sign-ins (time, address, browser) for 90 days, to spot break-in attempts.

No advertising, no trackers, no third-party scripts. This site is open source.
