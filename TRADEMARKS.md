# ArkAngel name and logo

The ArkAngel **code** is open source under the [Apache License 2.0](LICENSE). Copy it, change it and
run it, as that license says.

The **name** "ArkAngel" (also written "Ark Angel") and the **logo** (the amber map pin under a halo)
are different. Section 6 of the license says it grants no rights to use them. They belong to
NullSecurity LLC, and they exist so people can tell the real project from a copy. That matters here
more than for most software: ArkAngel holds tips about missing people and, in principal-defender
cases, the addresses of people hiding from someone. A copy that passes itself off as ArkAngel could
harvest exactly that.

## What's fine

- Saying truthfully that your project is **based on** ArkAngel, or **compatible** with it.
- Running an **unmodified** official release under its own name, on your own website, for your own
  cases. Please still say clearly who runs the site: visitors should be able to tell which
  organization they're sending tips to.
- Using the name to write about, review or teach the project.

## What needs your own name and logo

- A **fork** or any **modified build** you distribute, including a rebuilt browser extension.
- Any website, app or extension that would make someone think it comes from, or is endorsed by, the
  ArkAngel project when it doesn't.

If you're unsure, or want to ask permission, open an issue or reach the maintainers through the
repository.

## How to check you have the real thing

See "Is this the real ArkAngel?" in the [README](README.md): every official release file carries a
signed build attestation that `gh attestation verify` checks. To report a copy that pretends to be
ArkAngel, use the private [security advisory form](https://github.com/NullSecurityLLC/Ark_Angel/security/advisories/new).
